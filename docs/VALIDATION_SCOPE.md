# Validation scope

The functional taxonomy and classification prompt were developed with input
from legal experts. The repository also contains construct-validation outputs
for instruments with formally assigned Lamfalussy levels and exposes the text,
span classifications, and evidence needed for qualitative inspection.

These checks do not constitute an independent multi-expert gold standard at
clause level. The stored outputs cannot support a retrospective claim of
inter-annotator agreement, precision, recall, or expert-validated accuracy
across the entire procurement corpus.

`data/review/independent_annotation_sample.csv` is a balanced review worksheet
prepared for future use. Its expert and adjudication fields are intentionally
blank. If two or more experts later complete it independently, their labels can
be used to report agreement and performance without changing the archived
model outputs.
